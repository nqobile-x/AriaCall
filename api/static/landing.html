HOW IT WORKS

01

Ask anything

Type your question or tap the mic to speak. Aria understands natural language — no need to use keywords or fill out a form. You can also start a full voice call.

02

Aria searches

Aria queries the PulseFlow knowledge base using semantic search — finding the most relevant answers even when your wording doesn't match exactly. Your company's uploaded docs are included.

03

Resolved or escalated

If Aria can answer, you get it instantly. If the issue needs a human, Aria collects your name and email, creates a ticket, and sends you a confirmation — all without leaving the chat.

WHAT ARIA CAN DO

BILLING

Billing & payments

Answer questions about charges, invoices, subscription plans, refunds, and payment methods. Aria can explain line items and spot duplicate charges.

ACCOUNT

Account management

Help with password resets, profile updates, access issues, and account verification. Aria looks up account status in real time.

TECHNICAL

Technical support

Guide users through common technical issues — dashboard problems, integration errors, performance issues — using your knowledge base and uploaded documentation.

ESCALATION

Human handoff

When a query is too complex for Aria, it escalates gracefully — collecting contact details, creating a support ticket, and sending a confirmation email to the user.

VOICE

Voice-to-voice calls

Users can start a full voice call — Aria greets them, listens via speech recognition, and speaks responses aloud using neural TTS. Hands-free support that feels like a real call.

KNOWLEDGE

Your docs, instantly

Companies can upload PDFs, Word docs, or text files to the admin panel. Aria indexes them immediately with semantic search — your policies, guides, and manuals become searchable in seconds.
